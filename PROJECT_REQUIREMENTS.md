Retail Sales Performance Analysis - Project Requirements

Business: Multi-Store Retail Company  
Period:1 January 2025 to 31 December 2025

Business Objective

Management wants to understand sales performance, customer behavior, product profitability, store performance, and sales-channel performance.

The objective is to clean the raw data, prepare a reliable analytical model, analyze the data, and create an interactive executive Excel dashboard.

 Data Sources

- customers.csv
- products.csv
- stores.csv
- orders.csv
- order_items.csv

The datasets may contain data-quality issues that should be identified and handled appropriately.

Required Work:

1. Data Cleaning

- Remove duplicate customer records.
- Standardize inconsistent text and capitalization.
- Handle missing and blank values.
- Check product prices and discount values.
- Validate dates, quantities, and order status.
- Document important cleaning decisions.

2. Data Model

Create relationships between:

- Customers to Orders
- Stores to Orders
- Orders to Order Items
- Products to Order Items

3. Calculated Metrics

Calculate:

- Gross Sales
- Discount Amount
- Net Sales
- Product Cost
- Profit
- Profit Margin percentage
- Number of Orders
- Units Sold
- Average Order Value
- Number of Customers

4. Business Analysis

Analyze:

- Overall sales and profit
- Monthly sales trends
- Category performance
- Product performance
- Store performance
- Sales-channel performance
- Customer-segment performance
- Regional performance

5. Executive Dashboard

Create one interactive Excel dashboard containing:

 KPIs

- Net Sales
- Net Profit
- Profit Margin
- Orders
- Customers
- Average Order Value

 Visualizations

- Monthly Sales Trend
- Sales by Category
- Profit by Category
- Top 10 Products
- Store Performance
- Sales Channel Performance
- Customer Segment Performance
- Profit Margin by Store

 Slicers

- Month
- Category
- Region
- Store
- Sales Channel
- Customer Segment

6. Final Output

The final solution should include:

- Cleaned datasets
- Analytical data model
- Calculated metrics
- Interactive dashboard
- Key business insights
- Business recommendations
